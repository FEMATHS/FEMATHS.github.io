高阶微分方程的降阶​

在初值问题中，odesolver只能用来数值求解一阶微分方程(组)。对于高阶微分方程,可以将其化成一阶微分方程组，进而用odesolver求解，过程如下。

有 nnn 阶微分方程:

dnu dxn=f(x,u, du dx,…, dn−1u dxn−1)\frac{\mathrm{d}^{n} u}{\mathrm{~d} x^{n}}=f\left(x, u, \frac{\mathrm{~d} u}{\mathrm{~d} x}, \ldots, \frac{\mathrm{~d}^{n-1} u}{\mathrm{~d} x^{n-1}}\right) dxndnu​=f(x,u, dx du​,…, dxn−1 dn−1u​)

其在 x0x_{0}x0​ 处的初始条件为：

{u(x0)=η1u′(x0)=η2⋮u(n−1)(x0)=ηn\left\{\begin{array}{c} u\left(x_{0}\right)=\eta_{1} \\ u^{\prime}\left(x_{0}\right)=\eta_{2} \\ \vdots \\ u^{(n-1)}\left(x_{0}\right)=\eta_{n} \end{array}\right.⎩⎨⎧​u(x0​)=η1​u′(x0​)=η2​⋮u(n−1)(x0​)=ηn​​

做变量代换：

{u1=uu2=u′⋮un=u(n−1)\left\{\begin{array}{c} u_{1}=u \\ u_{2}=u^{\prime} \\ \vdots \\ u_{n}=u^{(n-1)} \end{array}\right.⎩⎨⎧​u1​=uu2​=u′⋮un​=u(n−1)​

{u1(x0)=η1u2(x0)=η2⋮un(x0)=ηn\left\{\begin{array}{c} u_{1}\left(x_{0}\right)=\eta_{1} \\ u_{2}\left(x_{0}\right)=\eta_{2} \\ \vdots \\ u_{n}\left(x_{0}\right)=\eta_{n} \end{array}\right.⎩⎨⎧​u1​(x0​)=η1​u2​(x0​)=η2​⋮un​(x0​)=ηn​​
